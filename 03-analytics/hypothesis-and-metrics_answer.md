## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** Mara, 38, the Consumer
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** Resolve her claims independently through a simple, trustworthy, and flexible self-service journey
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** Mara has two open claims. Her income is stable but varies with her shift work, and she cannot pay the full amount at once. After receiving a digital reminder, she opens the portal on her phone during a short break. She is unsure whether she qualifies for an instalment plan, what monthly payment would be accepted, what happens if she misses a payment, and whether both claims can be covered together. When the journey becomes confusing, displays errors, times out, or requests information she cannot easily provide, she abandons the self-service flow and contacts customer support instead.
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** Google searches for instalment plans, debt collection information, or payment consequences Notes app or paper notes to calculate an affordable monthly amount Banking app to check account balances and upcoming expenses
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** Willing payers are dropping out of our instalment journey, keeping conversion rates low, increasing cost per claim, reducing revenue, and eroding trust among both consumers and clients.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** By empowering consumers to resolve their claims independently through a seamless self-service experience, Riverty can increase payment recovery rates, reduce servicing costs, and strengthen trust with both consumers and clients. Launching this initiative now is critical to prevent revenue loss and growing operational inefficiencies.
