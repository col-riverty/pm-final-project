# Future State Journey: Mara’s Self-Service Instalment Experience

**Persona:** Mara, 38, the Consumer  
**Goal:** Resolve her claims independently through a simple, trustworthy, and flexible self-service journey.  
**Strategy:** Reclaim operational simplicity by removing legacy noise and becoming fastest for everyday work.

## One-Page Visual Timeline

**Digital reminder** → **1. Understand Eligibility** → **2. Personalize Plan** → **3. Confirm Arrangement** → **4. Stay on Track** → **Claims managed independently**

---

## 1. Understand Eligibility

- **User Action:** Open claims overview → See eligible claims and available options instantly.
- **Internal State:** Understand clear choices → Feel confident enough to continue independently.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Replace unclear eligibility → Prevent redirects, uncertainty, and early abandonment.

## 2. Personalize the Plan

- **User Action:** Adjust instalment amount → Receive affordable, valid options immediately.
- **Internal State:** Compare transparent scenarios → Feel informed and financially in control.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Explain amounts and consequences → Eliminate external research and support calls.

## 3. Confirm the Arrangement

- **User Action:** Review and approve digitally → Create the arrangement within minutes.
- **Internal State:** Receive clear confirmation → Trust that both claims are correctly managed.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Stabilize submission and validation → Prevent errors, restarts, and payment drop-off.

## 4. Stay on Track

- **User Action:** Resume and monitor payments → Manage changes without repeating information.
- **Internal State:** Track progress and consequences → Remain confident after setup.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Preserve progress across sessions → Prevent timeouts, abandonment, and agent escalation.

---

## Competitive Advantages over the Manual Workaround

1. **Instant clarity → Faster completion than Google research, phone calls, or email exchanges.**
2. **Guided flexibility → Higher confidence without spreadsheets, guesswork, or repeated explanations.**
3. **Reliable self-service → Higher conversion with lower agent workload and cost per claim.**
