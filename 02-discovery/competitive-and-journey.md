# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Persona 1:  Mara, 38, the Consumer
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Mara wants to resolve her claims independently through a simple and trustworthy self-service process. She needs a flexible instalment plan that fits her financial situation without having to call customer support or explain her circumstances to an agent.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Mara has two open claims. Her income is stable but varies with her shift work, and she cannot pay the full amount at once. After receiving a digital reminder, she opens the portal on her phone during a short break. She is unsure whether she qualifies for an instalment plan, what monthly payment would be accepted, what happens if she misses a payment, and whether both claims can be covered together. When the journey becomes confusing, displays errors, times out, or requests information she cannot easily provide, she abandons the self-service flow and contacts customer support instead.
