One-Click Payment Plan Setup

Author: Carlos Olivera · Status: Draft · Target: High-Fidelity Prototype

1 · The Big Picture

Vision

Enable consumers with stable income and an opened case to create a clear, affordable payment plan in one mobile-first session without abandoning the resolution flow or contacting support.

Press release

Consumer Resolution Velocity introduces One-Click Payment Plan Setup, a faster path from an opened case to an active repayment agreement.

Consumers can select an eligible payment plan, review the complete repayment schedule, and confirm the agreement in one streamlined digital flow. Clear installment amounts, due dates, and total repayment information remove the uncertainty that currently causes consumers to leave before completing a resolution.

The experience targets the drop from 49% starting a self-service flow to only 31% successfully resolving an issue. By keeping eligibility, plan selection, confirmation, and success feedback in one session, it aims to increase Digital Resolution Rate while protecting the existing mobile consumer base.

2 · The Details

User stories

US1

Determine eligibility

As a consumer with stable income and an opened case, I want to know immediately whether I am eligible for a payment plan, so that I do not spend time entering a flow I cannot complete.

Acceptance criteria

  • Eligibility is evaluated when the payment-plan entry point is selected.
  • An eligible consumer reaches plan selection without re-entering case information.
  • An ineligible consumer receives a clear reason and an available next action.
  • No plan is shown unless the consumer can confirm it.
  • Eligibility messaging avoids legal or technical terminology.
US2

Select and understand a payment plan

As a qualifying consumer, I want to compare available installment options and understand their consequences, so that I can choose an affordable schedule with confidence.

Acceptance criteria

  • Each option shows installment amount, frequency, first due date, number of installments, and total repayment.
  • The selected option is visually distinct.
  • The repayment summary updates immediately when the selection changes.
  • No fee, amount, or due date is hidden behind expandable content.
  • Confirmation remains unavailable until one valid option is selected.
US3

Confirm the agreement

As a consumer who selected an affordable plan, I want to confirm it without offline steps or repeated data entry, so that I can resolve my case in the same session.

Acceptance criteria

  • The consumer confirms from the plan-selection screen.
  • One explicit action is required after the final summary is visible.
  • Scrolling or navigation cannot trigger confirmation.
  • Success shows the next payment date, installment amount, and reference number.
  • Failure preserves the selected plan and provides a retry path.
  • No success state appears without the predefined success condition.

Screens to build (exactly three)

1 Entry

Resolve your case

Purpose: Confirm case context and move an eligible consumer directly into payment-plan selection.

  • Case reference, outstanding balance, and case status
  • Short payment-plan description and eligibility status
  • Set up a payment plan primary CTA
  • View other options secondary action
  • Eligibility notice, loading state, and ineligible state
  • Mobile navigation header
2 Core

Choose your payment plan

Purpose: Select, understand, and confirm a plan.

  • Step 1 of 2 progress label
  • Two or three selectable installment cards
  • Installment amount, frequency, first date, count, and total repayment
  • Selected-plan indicator and real-time summary
  • Required agreement checkbox
  • Confirm payment plan and Back
  • Inline validation, processing state, and plain-language error banner
3 Success

Your payment plan is active

Purpose: Remove uncertainty after confirmation and make the next action explicit.

  • Success indicator and confirmation reference
  • Installment amount, frequency, next date, total repayment, and schedule summary
  • Explanation of what happens next
  • Return to case overview and View payment-plan details
  • Support path for incorrect details
  • Confirmation-state identifier for evaluation

Functional requirements

FR1 · Eligibility gating: Classify the prototype consumer as eligible or ineligible before showing selectable plans.
FR2 · Plan availability: Show two to three predefined options with amount, frequency, first due date, installment count, and total repayment.
FR3 · Complete cost visibility: Total repayment must equal the displayed installments; no additional amount may appear after confirmation.
FR4 · Immediate summary update: Update the summary within 300 milliseconds after a plan change.
FR5 · Confirmation control: Disable confirmation until a valid selection, accepted agreement, and valid plan data are present.
FR6 · Single-session completion: Complete the journey within the three-screen experience without repeated case information.
FR7 · Error recovery: Preserve selection and agreement state after failure, explain the issue, and provide same-screen retry.
FR8 · Mobile usability: Remain usable at 375 px without horizontal scrolling or ambiguous navigation.

Technical constraints

Build within these constraints
  • React functional components
  • useState only
  • Predefined local mock data
  • Exactly three user-facing screens
  • Local loading, success, ineligible, validation-error, and confirmation-error states
  • Deterministic calculations from fixture data
Do not build
  • No external APIs or backend
  • No login, database, or persistence
  • No real payment processing or contract creation
  • No AI or generative functionality
  • No external state library
  • No routing framework unless required
  • No analytics, document upload, support-agent integration, or production eligibility engine
This prototype tests transactional friction, not financial decisioning. If required fixture data is missing or inconsistent, block confirmation rather than estimating a value.

3 · The Logistics

Features out

  • AI translation of legal language
  • Personalized claim explanations
  • Interactive claim Q&A
  • Multilingual AI assistance
  • Generative case summaries
  • Full Guided Resolution Wizard
  • Resume across sessions
  • Smart Document Upload, camera capture, validation, missing-document detection, and preview

Edge cases

  • Eligibility unavailable: show a neutral unavailable state; do not estimate plan options.
  • No plans available: explain that a plan cannot currently be configured and offer View other options.
  • Invalid repayment data: block confirmation.
  • No selection: keep confirmation disabled and identify the next required action.
  • Agreement not accepted: keep confirmation disabled and associate the validation message with the checkbox.
  • Confirmation failure: preserve the plan and agreement state and show retry.
  • Repeated confirmation: reject duplicates while processing.
  • Small screen: stack cards vertically at 375 px.
  • Long or localized values: wrap or truncate safely without overlap.
  • Safety and hallucination guard: display only predefined eligibility, amounts, fees, dates, and contractual wording; block confirmation when data is unavailable or inconsistent.

Decision log

1 · Payment-plan completion over broad resolution guidance.

Build the direct payment-plan path instead of the full Guided Resolution Wizard. It is the shortest path to Digital Resolution Rate and avoids extra choices that may increase abandonment.

Scope protected: exactly three screens, one resolution outcome, and no alternate end-to-end journeys.

2 · Deterministic fixture data over AI explanation.

Use predefined case and plan data. AI would add complexity and financial-content safety risk while making prototype outcomes harder to attribute.

Scope protected: no external APIs, generated financial information, claim explanation, or recommendation model.

Evals

Flow accuracy: at least 95% of test runs preserve the selected plan, installment amount, first due date, total repayment, and reference across Core and Success. Any mismatch or invented value is a failure.
Time on task: at least 80% of eligible participants complete setup in three minutes or less without moderator help; no more than 25% abandon before Success.
Safety triggers: 100% of tests with missing, inconsistent, or unavailable financial data block confirmation. Zero tolerance for invented amounts, changed totals, unsupported eligibility, false success, hidden information, or confirmation with invalid data.
