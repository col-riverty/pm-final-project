MUST HAVE

One-Click Payment Plan Setup

If this capability is removed, the core persona cannot efficiently complete the primary resolution action. Therefore, every item below is required to move the Digital Resolution Rate.

M1. Eligibility Check

• Determine whether the consumer qualifies for a payment plan.

• Prevent users from entering flows they cannot complete.

M2. Payment Plan Selection

• Present available installment options.

• Allow users to select a preferred repayment schedule.

M3. Real-Time Payment Summary

• Show installment amount, frequency, due dates, and total repayment.

• Reduce uncertainty before commitment.

M4. One-Click Confirmation

• Confirm agreement without additional offline steps.

• Complete the resolution within the same session.

M5. Success Confirmation Screen

• Clearly communicate that the payment plan is active.

• Show next payment date and reference number.

M6. Error & Validation Handling

• Explain failures in simple language.

• Keep consumers inside the flow rather than forcing support contact.

M7. Mobile-Optimized Experience

• Complete the full setup process on mobile devices.

• Support the dominant consumer usage pattern.

SHOULD HAVE

Guided Resolution Wizard

Strongly improves completion rates and reduces abandonment, but consumers can still complete a payment plan without it.

S1. Resolution Path Discovery

• Identify the most relevant resolution option for the consumer's case.

S2. Step-by-Step Navigation

• Break complex resolution processes into manageable steps.

S3. Progress Visibility

• Show current step and remaining steps.

S4. Contextual Guidance

• Explain what information is required at each stage.

S5. Resume Interrupted Flow

• Allow consumers to continue where they left off.

COULD HAVE

Smart Document Upload

Valuable friction reduction, but not necessary for the first release if payment-plan setup is the primary objective.

C1. Drag-and-Drop Upload

C2. Mobile Camera Capture

C3. File Validation

C4. Missing Document Detection

C5. Upload Status Feedback

C6. Document Preview

WON'T HAVE (NOW)

AI Claim Explanation

Helpful for comprehension but not required to solve the core problem in the first release.

W1. AI Translation of Legal Language

W2. Personalized Claim Explanations

W3. Interactive Claim Q&A

W4. Multilingual AI Assistance

W5. Generative Summary of Collection Cases
