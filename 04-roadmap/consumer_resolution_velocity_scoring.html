Effort vs. Value Assessment

Senior PM Scoring

Scenario C · Consumer Resolution Velocity

Strategic Anchors

PersonaConsumers with stable income and opened cases
Primary MetricDigital Resolution Rate
Moment of MiseryConsumers Abandoning Resolution Flow
GuardrailMonthly Active Consumers

Scoring Logic

Value reflects expected impact on Digital Resolution Rate and reduction of Consumers Abandoning Resolution Flow.

Effort assumes the stated team and quarterly delivery horizon.

Quadrants: Quick Win = high value / low effort; Major Project = high value / high effort; Fill-In = low value / low effort; Time Sinker = low value / high effort.

Constraints

  • Team size: 4 people
  • 3 developers + 1 QA
  • 2-week sprints
  • Quarterly roadmap planning: 4

Priority Ranking

Tier 1 - Build First (Quarter 1)

  1. C3 One-Click Payment Plan Setup
  2. C2 Guided Resolution Wizard
  3. C6 Smart Document Upload
  4. C4 Resolution Progress Tracker
  5. C13 Resolution Confidence Indicator
  6. C1 AI Claim Explanation

These features attack the exact moment of misery: Consumers Abandoning Resolution Flow.

Tier 2 - Build Next

  1. C8 Proactive Resolution Notifications
  2. C10 Search-to-Resolution Experience
  3. C12 One-Click Dispute Submission
  4. C7 Contextual Help & FAQ

They increase completion and recovery of partially completed journeys.

Tier 3 - Strategic Investments

  1. C14 Mobile-First Self-Service Dashboard
  2. C5 Personalized Resolution Recommendations
  3. C9 Consumer Trust Dashboard

Valuable, but unlikely to outperform Tier 1 features in the next roadmap cycle.

Tier 4 - Avoid for Now

  1. C15 Support Escalation Assistant
  2. C11 AI Chat Resolution Assistant

Both consume meaningful capacity while contributing only indirectly to the primary metric.

Ruthless PM Takeaway

If capacity is limited to six features in one quarter, fund C2, C3, C4, C6, C1, and C13. Together they directly reduce abandonment, increase consumer confidence, and remove friction from the highest-value resolution paths.
