MoSCoW scoping promptAct as a Senior Product Manager at Riverty Collection. I'm about to write a
PRD for my selected feature:  Consumer Resolution Velocity.

Use the Persona, Moment of Misery, Primary Metric, and Constraints defined earlier
to guide your decisions.

List the sub-requirements for this feature in MoSCoW categories:
- MUST HAVE: One-Click Payment Plan Setup
- SHOULD HAVE: Guided Resolution Wizard
- COULD HAVE: Smart Document Upload
- WON'T HAVE (NOW):  AI Claim Explanation

Be ruthless. Every must-have must directly address the moment of misery. If removing
it still lets the core persona Consumers with stable income and opened cases it's not a must-have.
